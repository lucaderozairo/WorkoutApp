Design notes
  • Route drawn on Leaflet map (already in project). Click to add waypoints, distance auto-calculated.
  • Saved routes tab shows previously completed or saved routes as a list to select from.
  • Swim has no map step — just pool length, target distance, and target pace per 100m.
  • Distance markers calculated as: cumulative time = distance × pace. Interval: every 1 km (run/swim) or every 5 km (cycle).
  • Planned session stored with type, name, date/time, exercises or route+pace, markers.
  • Schedule screen picks up planned sessions by date.
